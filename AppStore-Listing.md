## Description (4000 chars max)

Snap & Scan Keeper is a powerful yet simple document scanner for iPhone and iPad. Capture documents, receipts, notes, and more with your device camera and choose exactly how to save them.

**Four Flexible Save Modes:**

• Share Only – Scan and share immediately via Messages, Mail, Files, or AirDrop without saving to Photos
• PDF Only – Save scans as a single combined PDF document
• Individual Pages + PDF – Save both individual page images and a combined PDF
• Pages Only – Save scans as individual images without creating a PDF

**Smart Organization:**

Scans saved to Photos are automatically organized into dedicated albums:
• "Snap&ScanKeeper PDFs" for PDF documents
• "Snap&ScanKeeper Images" for individual page images

**Key Features:**

• Native iOS document camera powered by VisionKit
• Automatic edge detection and perspective correction
• Multi-page scanning support
• Instant PDF generation
• Share sheet integration (Messages, Mail, Files, AirDrop, and more)
• Custom Photos albums for easy organization
• User-configurable save preferences
• Clean, simple interface
• No ads, no subscriptions, no data collection
• Works completely offline

**Privacy First:**

Snap & Scan Keeper respects your privacy. All scanning and processing happens on your device. We never collect, store, or transmit your documents or personal information. Your scans are yours alone.

**Perfect For:**

• Digitizing receipts and invoices
• Scanning contracts and documents
• Capturing notes and whiteboards
• Archiving important papers
• Quick document sharing
• Creating digital backups

**How It Works:**

1. Tap "Scan Document" to open the camera
2. Capture your document (auto-detects edges and corrects perspective)
3. Add more pages if needed
4. Choose how to save in Settings
5. Done! Share or find in Photos albums

**Requirements:**

• iOS 16.0 or later
• Camera access for document scanning
• Photos access for saving scans (optional - only needed if saving to Photos)

Download Snap & Scan Keeper today and experience the easiest way to digitize your documents!

## App Review Information

**Notes for Reviewer:**
Snap & Scan Keeper is a document scanner that uses iOS VisionKit framework. The app requires camera permission to scan documents and Photos permission to save scans (optional, only if user chooses a save mode that writes to Photos).

The default mode is "Share Only" which creates PDFs without saving to Photos - perfect for quick sharing via Messages, Mail, or Files.

**Demo Instructions:**
1. Launch app
2. Tap "Scan Document"
3. Grant camera permission when prompted
4. Point camera at any document or paper
5. App will auto-detect edges - tap capture button
6. Share sheet will appear automatically (default "Share Only" mode)
7. To test Photos saving: Tap gear icon (top right) → Choose "PDF Only" → Scan again → Grant Photos permission

No demo account needed - all features work immediately.
